LETI v11 — MOBILE NAVIGATION AND DAILY READING
==============================================

Changes:
- The top header now reserves vertical space on all non-home pages, so the floating logo no longer overlaps the chapter/story navigation.
- On narrow screens, navigation buttons stack vertically; on wider screens they sit in two equal columns.
- The story page keeps separate actions: "К главе" and "На главную".
- Chapter pages show "Прочитано X из Y опубликованных историй".
- Each story card shows its status: completed, available, or locked.
- Reading is sequential: the first published story is available immediately. Completing a story marks it done; the next published story becomes available on the next local calendar day. Previously completed stories can always be reopened.
- Progress and completion timestamps are stored in this browser on this device.

Important:
- The current manuscript has only the stories actually published in the app available for sequential progression (currently Days 1 and 2). Placeholder chapter entries are not fabricated.
- Existing progress is preserved in localStorage unless site data is cleared.
- After deployment, hard-refresh the site (Ctrl+Shift+R on desktop; on iPhone, close/reopen the tab and refresh). The service worker cache name was updated to v11.
